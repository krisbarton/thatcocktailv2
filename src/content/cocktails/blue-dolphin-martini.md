### Blue Curaçao

[Blue Curaçao](/alcohol-recommendations/#blue-curacao) is a liqueur flavored with the dried peel of the Laraha citrus fruit, which is then artificially coloured blue for effect.

It’s a great ingredient to use in cocktails because it pretty much has everything you could ever want as a cocktail mixer:

1.  It has an enticing colour.
2.  It has a bitter orange taste that blends well with most flavours.
3.  It has a great amount of alcohol in it.

### Peach Schnapps

[Peach Schnapps](/alcohol-recommendations/#peach-schnapps) is a peach-flavoured varaint of the Schnapps alcoholic drink.

Schnapps may take several forms:

-   Distilled Fruit Brandy.
-   Herbal Liqueurs.
-   Infusions – i.e. the process of extracting flavours from plant material in solvent such as water, oil, or in the case of Schnapps – alcohol.
-   Flavour Liqueurs – this is where Peach Schnapps comes in.

The word *Schnapps* is a German term that refers to any kind of strong Alcoholic drink and over the years has become known as a style of drink. [More about that here](/what-is-peach-schnapps/).

### Mixing Tips

-   Chill the glass while you shake: fill it with ice and water, then tip it out just before you strain.
-   Blue curaçao is an orange liqueur coloured blue, so it brings a citrus note as well as the colour.
-   Shake hard. A properly cold tin is what makes it taste smooth rather than sweet.
