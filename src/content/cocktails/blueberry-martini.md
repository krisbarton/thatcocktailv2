method:
  - 'Muddle 8 blueberries in the base of your <a href="/cocktail-equipment/#cocktail-shaker">cocktail shaker</a> until they’re liquid.'
  - Add ice to the shaker.
  - 'Use a <a href="/cocktail-equipment/#jigger">jigger</a> to measure the <a href="/alcohol-recommendations/#vodka">vodka</a>, <a href="/alcohol-recommendations/#triple-sec">triple sec</a>, lime juice and simple syrup into the shaker.'
  - Shake until cold to the touch.
  - Strain into a martini glass.
  - Garnish with a skewer of 3 blueberries.
