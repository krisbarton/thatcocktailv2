The Cheeky Monkey Cocktail is a little different from our usual mixes.

Sure, it’s still quick and easy to make, but it has a rather niche ingredient in it: **Yellow Chartreuse**.

With Yellow Chartreuse, you mix Vodka, Orange Juice, and Orange Bitters to make a tasty but rather *out-there* blend.

You just have to try it!

*How do you make a Cheeky Monkey? What even is Yellow Chartreuse…?*

Great questions! Let’s get into it!

## What’s a Cheeky Monkey Cocktail?

*What’s a Cheeky Monkey?*

…is the exact question I thought that ran through my mind when I saw the cocktail on a menu of a swanky cocktail bar some years back.

I’d never heard of it before and I love trying new cocktails.

Then I looked at the ingredients: **Vodka, Yellow Chartreuse, Orange Juice, and Orange Bitters…**

…wait. *What even is Yellow Chartreuse?* (you can find out more about Yellow Chartreuse below).

It didn’t matter. I ordered the cocktail and **I loved it**. The flavour was so different: orange citrus overtones, resting on strong herbal undertones.

It was fantastic – you have to try it.

Before we get into the recipe, here are some tips and tricks to take the cocktail to the next level.

### Mixing Tips

-   Yellow Chartreuse is the softer, sweeter, honeyed sister of the green. It rounds the orange out rather than taking over.
-   Squeeze the oranges fresh; the juice is the biggest part of the drink.
-   One dash of bitters is plenty: it sharpens the orange and dries the finish.
-   Chill the glass first, as the drink is served straight up with no ice.
