method:
  - 'Add ice to your <a href="/cocktail-equipment/#cocktail-shaker">cocktail shaker</a>.'
  - Pour in your rum and cider.
  - Squeeze in the juice of half of the lime.
  - Shake well.
  - 'Pour into an <a href="/cocktail-glassware/#old-fashioned-glasses">Old Fashioned glass</a>.'
  - Top up with ginger beer.
  - Cut up the other half of the lime into wedges and garnish.
