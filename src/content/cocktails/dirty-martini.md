This is a dirty flex on the [classic Vodka Martini recipe](/cocktails/vodka-martini/), in which we add the brine from the green olive garnish to give the drink an extra, savoury dimension.

From experience, this is one people either love or hate, but it’s always an experience.

### Vermouth

Vermouth is an aromatic fortified wine that’s flavoured with various botanicals and is sometimes coloured.

Traditionally, Vermouth was used for medicinal purposes (we’ve heard that one before, am I right guys?) but was later served as an aperitif in fashionable Turin cafes in the mid to late 18th century.

By the 19th century, it’d become popular with bartenders in the creation of fancy cocktails such as the [Martini](/vesper-martini-recipe/), the [Manhattan](/how-to-make-a-manhattan/), the Rob Roy, and the Negroni.
