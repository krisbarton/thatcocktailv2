faqs:
  - q: What’s the difference between a Kentucky Mule and a Moscow Mule?
    a: >-
      Just the spirit: a Kentucky Mule swaps the vodka in a <a href="/cocktails/moscow-mule/">Moscow Mule</a> for bourbon. Everything else, from the ginger beer to the copper mug, stays the same.
---

You just swap out the Vodka for Bourbon and voila, you have your Kentucky Mule.

I’m a massive fan of Bourbon and a massive fan of the Moscow Mule – I didn’t think those two worlds should ever mix. But I tried it anyway and I don’t think I’ll go back. You won’t either.
