The Mint Julep is a drink that’s been around since the 18th Century and is firmly rooted as a classic.

Like most classics, it’s only got a couple of ingredients, it’s easy to make, and it’s boozey yet sweet.

You make it by mixing Mint Leaves, Bourbon, and Simple Syrup.

## What Ingredients do you Need to Make a Mint Julep?

You just need Bourbon, Simple Syrup, and Mint Leaves.

### Bourbon

[Bourbon](/alcohol-recommendations/#bourbon) is a type of Whiskey. It was created in America and is a barrel-aged distilled liquor that’s made primarily from corn.

The name Bourbon originates from the [French Bourbon Dynasty,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Bourbon) but how this type of American whiskey got that name is up for debate.

Major contenders include:

-   Bourbon Street in New Orleans
-   Bourbon County in Kentucky

…with the latter the reason why Bourbon is so closely connected to Kentucky.

Interestingly, by law, [Bourbon](/alcohol-recommendations/#bourbon) sold in the US must be produced in the US, be made from at least 51% corn, and stored in a container of charred oak.
