method:

  - 'Add ice, <a href="/alcohol-recommendations/#tequila">tequila</a>, and lime juice to your <a href="/cocktail-equipment/#cocktail-shaker">cocktail shaker</a> and then shake well until the outside of the shaker feels cold.'
  - 'Use a <a href="/cocktail-equipment/#hawthorne-strainer">Hawthorne strainer</a> to strain the ingredients into a <a href="/cocktail-glassware/#highball-glasses">highball glass</a>.'
  - Add the pink grapefruit soda and stir in with a bar spoon.
  - Garnish with a slice of lime.
