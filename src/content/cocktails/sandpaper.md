  - { item: grapefruit juice, ml: 60, oz: 2 }

  - { item: cherry brandy, ml: 30, oz: 1 }
method:
  - 'Add ice and all the ingredients to a <a href="/cocktail-equipment/#cocktail-shaker">cocktail shaker</a>.'
  - Shake well.
  - 'Use a <a href="/cocktail-equipment/#hawthorne-strainer">Hawthorne strainer</a> to strain into a <a href="/cocktail-glassware/#cocktail-glasses">cocktail glass</a>.'
tips:
  - Cherry brandy is a sweet cherry liqueur, rich and fruity, with notes of marzipan and almond from the crushed cherry stones.
  - Pink grapefruit gives the drink its rosy colour; white grapefruit makes it a little sharper.
  - 'Chill the glass while you shake: fill it with ice and water, then tip it out just before you strain.'
