## 1. Introduction

Welcome to our website! This Cookie GDPR Policy explains how we use cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other relevant data protection laws.

## 2. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, smartphone) when you visit a website. They serve various purposes, such as remembering your preferences, analyzing website traffic, and enhancing your overall browsing experience.

## 3. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies on our website for the following purposes:

a) **Essential Cookies**: These cookies are necessary for the proper functioning of our website. They allow you to navigate through the site and use essential features, such as accessing secure areas. Without these cookies, some services on our website may not be available.

b) **Performance Cookies**: These cookies collect information about how visitors use our website, such as which pages they visit most frequently and if they encounter any error messages. The data collected is aggregated and anonymized, and it helps us improve the performance and usability of our website.

c) **Functionality Cookies**: Functionality cookies enable our website to remember choices you make (such as your username, language, or region) and provide enhanced, more personalized features. These cookies may also be used to remember changes you have made to text size, fonts, and other customizable elements.

d) **Targeting/Advertising Cookies**: We may use these cookies to deliver relevant advertisements to you based on your interests and online activities. They help us serve you with ads that are more likely to be meaningful to you and also limit the number of times you see an ad. These cookies are placed by third-party advertising networks with our permission.

## 4. Consent to Use Cookies

Upon your first visit to our website, you will be presented with a cookie banner seeking your consent to use non-essential cookies. By clicking “Accept” or continuing to use our website, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

## 5. Managing Cookies

If you wish to revoke your consent or manage cookies on your device, you can do so through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block cookies or alert you when a website attempts to place cookies on your device. Please note that blocking or deleting cookies may impact certain features and functionalities of our website.

## 6. Third-Party Cookies

Third-party services we use on our website may also place cookies on your device. We have no control over these cookies and encourage you to review the respective privacy policies of these third parties for more information about their cookie practices.

## 7. Data Protection and Privacy

We are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with our Privacy Policy. For more information about how we handle personal data, please refer to our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/).
